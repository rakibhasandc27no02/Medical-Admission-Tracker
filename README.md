# Medical Admission Pro

### **Prepare Smarter. Practice Harder. Perform Better.**

A complete medical admission preparation ecosystem built for students who want more than just a question bank.

**📚 Learn**
Explore subject-wise and year-wise medical MCQs, previous-year questions, answers, and explanations—at your own pace.

**🎯 Practice**
Build accuracy through focused practice sessions, review mistakes, strengthen weak subjects, and improve your preparation without competitive pressure.

**⚡ Challenge**
Take high-intensity medical quizzes with timed examinations, structured instructions, instant results, and competitive scoring.

**🏆 Compete**
Earn quiz points and compete on a global leaderboard powered by Firebase, with fast cached rankings and silent background updates.

**📊 Track Your Progress**
Monitor accuracy, practice performance, quiz history, solved questions, points, and overall academic progress from your personal dashboard.

**🌐 Your Language, Your Way**
Study in **বাংলা or English**, with language support across questions, options, explanations, instructions, results, and the complete interface.

**👤 Your Personal Learning Space**
Maintain your student profile, manage your academic information, access your learning activity, and keep your preparation organized in one place.

### **Learn → Practice → Challenge → Analyze → Improve**

**Medical Admission Pro** — built to turn consistent preparation into measurable progress and help students move closer to their medical admission goals.
